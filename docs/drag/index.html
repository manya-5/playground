Drag to archive

Drag a message left. Let go past the line, or flick it, and it's archived. Let go early and it springs back. Arrow keys work too.

Distance or speed

A message is archived if you drag it past 40% of its width or let go while moving fast. Short, quick flicks count.

Resistance past the edge

Dragging right has nowhere to go, so the card follows your finger at a third of the speed. You can feel the edge.

The color tells you first

The green shows up the moment letting go would archive, so there are no surprises when you release.
