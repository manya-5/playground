Toast with undo

Delete a few files. Each toast gives you five seconds to take it back, and the clock pauses while your pointer or focus is on it.

    Undo instead of "Are you sure?"

    Deleting happens right away, and the toast is the safety net. One click either way, and no dialog in the middle of your work.

    The clock waits for you

    Hovering or tabbing into a toast pauses its timer, so it won't disappear while you're reaching for Undo.

    Same words both ways

    "Deleted" in the toast, "Restored" after Undo. The action keeps one name the whole way through.
